Healthcare Problems in Pakistan

Challenges that everyday Pakistanis face

⏱️ Emergency Help Arrives Late

In many areas, especially rural, emergency medical help often arrives too late.

🏥 Lack of Doctors & Healthcare

Many regions lack sufficient doctors, hospitals, and basic healthcare facilities.

👴 Home-bound & Elderly Patients

Patients who are elderly or home-bound cannot easily reach hospitals or clinics.

💊 Medicine & First Aid Unavailable

Medicines and first-aid resources are not readily available everywhere.

⚖️ Doctor-to-People Ratio

Pakistan has roughly 1 doctor for every 1,200 people; 70% of population lives in rural areas.

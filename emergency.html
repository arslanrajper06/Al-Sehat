Highway Emergency Cabins

“Every 50 Kilometers — A Promise of Life.”

📍 Location & Frequency

Emergency medical cabins installed every 50 km along major highways, serving as lifesaving points for travelers.

🚨 AI LifeSaver Emergency Button

Positioned near each cabin and on roadsides — a bright red “AI LifeSaver” button directly alerts the nearest emergency team, doctors, and cabin for instant action.

🤝 Corporate & Government Partnerships

Sponsored and maintained through partnerships with PSO, Shell, HBL, Rescue 1122, and National Highway Authority (NHA).

⚡ Power & Connectivity

Solar-powered and GPS-enabled cabins with 24/7 connectivity to ensure operations even in remote areas.

🧑‍⚕️ Trained Medical Presence

Operated by trained volunteers, paramedics, or on-call doctors to handle emergencies and coordinate ambulance arrivals.

🤖 AI-Guided Health Terminal

Interactive AI terminal connected to AI Sehat’s network, providing step-by-step first-aid guidance and instant doctor video consultations.

🧠 Smart Integration

Linked with AI Tracker and Smart Map System for real-time ambulance routing, accident pinpointing, and live monitoring.

🩺 Medical Equipment & Supplies

Each cabin is fully stocked with essential medicines, trauma kits, oxygen cylinders, and digital consultation tools for immediate first-aid treatment.
